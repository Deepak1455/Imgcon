Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

Your personal privacy is our highest priority at ImgCon (https://imgcon.online). Unlike traditional online tools, your uploaded images never leave your browser memory. All conversion, compression, resizing, watermarking, and EXIF cleaning operations execute 100% locally on your computer or phone using WebAssembly and Web Workers.

1 Image File Handling & Zero Server Storage

Our serverless architecture ensures complete confidentiality for your media assets:

  • We DO NOT upload your photos to external servers.
  • We DO NOT store or retain copies of your images anywhere.
  • We DO NOT access, inspect, or harvest data from your files.

When processing is complete, your converted or compressed file is downloaded directly from your browser's local memory to your device storage.

2 Cookies & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

ImgCon uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to keep our tools 100% free for everyone. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve personalized advertisements based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites across the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to ImgCon and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or AboutAds.info.

3 Log Files & Website Analytics

ImgCon utilizes standard web analytics services (Google Analytics 4) to monitor general non-personally identifiable traffic patterns (e.g., browser types, screen resolution, referring pages, device operating system, time spent). This anonymous statistical data helps us optimize site performance, PWA caching, and tool compatibility.

4 Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under global privacy regulations, including GDPR and CCPA, users have the right to privacy, data transparency, and control. Because ImgCon does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or user files, your data remains fully under your personal control at all times.
