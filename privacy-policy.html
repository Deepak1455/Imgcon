About ImgCon

The Story Behind ImgCon

Built by Deepak • Web Developer

ImgCon was born out of a simple necessity: the need for a lightning-fast, private image processing tool that operates directly inside the browser. In a digital world where personal photo privacy is constantly at risk, most online image converters force users to upload sensitive files to remote third-party servers. We decided to change that forever.

By harnessing modern web technologies like WebAssembly, Web Workers, and HTML5 Canvas API, ImgCon performs all complex image conversion, compression, resizing, watermarking, and EXIF cleaning locally on your machine. Your photos never leave your device memory—delivering desktop-grade processing speeds with zero privacy concerns.

100% Private

Your files are completely your own. ImgCon processes media locally in your browser with zero server uploads or remote tracking.

Blazing Fast

Multi-threaded Web Worker architecture executes conversions in milliseconds without server upload or download lag.

Works Offline (PWA)

Install ImgCon as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on your phone or PC and process images seamlessly offline.
