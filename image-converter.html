Complete Guide to Online Image Conversion

Image conversion is the process of changing a digital image file from one file format container to another (for example, converting PNG to WebP or HEIC to JPG). Choosing the right image format is critical for web page speed optimization, graphic design accuracy, and digital compatibility across different operating systems.

Supported Image Formats Comparison

Format Compression Type Transparency Support Best Used For

JPG / JPEG Lossy No Real-world Photography, Digital Art, Landscapes

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this image converter free to use?

Yes, ImgCon Image Converter is 100% free with no file size limits, subscriptions, or watermarks applied to your outputs.

Will converting PNG with transparency to JPG keep the transparent background?

No, the JPG file specification does not support alpha transparency. Transparent pixels will automatically be replaced with a solid background color. To preserve transparency, convert to WebP or AVIF instead.
